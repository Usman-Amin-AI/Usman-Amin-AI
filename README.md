## About Me

I build AI systems that turn complex workflows into intelligent software.

My focus is on the engineering layer between emerging AI capabilities and real-world applications — designing systems that can retrieve knowledge, use tools, reason through multi-step tasks, automate workflows, and operate with appropriate controls.

### Current Focus

- **Agentic AI & AI Agents**
- **Enterprise & Agentic RAG**
- **Intelligent Workflow Automation**
- **AI-powered Applications & SaaS**
- **AI System Architecture**
- **LLM Evaluation, Reliability & Observability**

I'm particularly interested in the problems that appear after the initial AI demo works:

- How do we make the system reliable?
- How should agents use tools safely?
- How do we retrieve the right information?
- How do we evaluate AI behavior?
- How do we control cost and latency?
- How do we integrate AI into an actual business workflow?

I learn by building.

My projects explore areas such as **Agentic RAG, multimodal AI, AI agents, MCP-based systems, AI developer tools, voice AI, natural-language data interfaces, and intelligent automation**.

I document what I build, the engineering decisions behind it, what fails, and what I learn along the way.

> **My goal:** Build AI systems that are not just impressive in a demo, but useful, reliable, and engineered for real-world use.
